FAQ

Questions hiring teams ask.

What is an AI Content Strategist?

An AI Content Strategist plans what a brand should publish, why, and for whom, then uses AI tools to research, create and test that content faster. The strategy is human. AI speeds up the work. Maher Khan uses this approach across social media, video, email and ads.

Is Maher Khan open to full-time roles?

Yes. Maher Khan is open to full-time AI Content Strategist, Senior Content Strategist and Social Media Strategist roles in the San Francisco Bay Area, hybrid or remote. She is authorized to work in the United States and does not need visa sponsorship.

What AI tools does Maher Khan use?

Maher Khan uses Adobe Express, Adobe Firefly, Photoshop and Illustrator for creative work, Claude and ChatGPT for research and drafts, and n8n, Make and Claude Code to build workflows and light no-code apps for clients.

How is an AI Content Strategist different from a content strategist?

Both plan content around business goals. An AI Content Strategist also designs the AI workflow behind it: which tasks AI handles, which stay human, and how the team checks quality. The result is more content, tested faster, without losing the brand voice.

Can Maher Khan train a marketing team to use AI?

Yes. Maher Khan designed the AI Marketing Implementation course at UCLA Extension, where 15 professionals shipped their own AI agents. She is a former Assistant Professor of Marketing and has trained 20,000+ creators on design tools.

Does Maher Khan need visa sponsorship?

No. Maher Khan is authorized to work in the United States and does not need visa sponsorship, now or in the future. She is based in San Jose, California, and is open to full-time roles in the Bay Area, hybrid or remote.

What would Maher Khan do in her first 90 days?

In her first 30 days, Maher Khan audits content, channels and data and finds quick wins. By day 60 she builds content pillars, a calendar and a reusable AI workflow. By day 90 she ships, tests and reports what worked and what comes next.
