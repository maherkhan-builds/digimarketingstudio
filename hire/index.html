Education · AISep 2025 to Sep 2026 · Curriculum Content Developer and Contributing Instructor

UCLA Extension.

AI Marketing Implementation: Automation, Agentic and AI-Driven SEO

Why they asked me

I was a top student in Antonio Pagano's UCLA Extension course. After it ended, he invited me to co-create a course that takes working marketers from using AI to building with it.

The learners were professionals with jobs, not engineers. Each of them had to ship a working AI agent by the end of the term.

What I delivered

  • The full 11-week curriculum, from AI agent design to AI-driven SEO
  • The AI Fluency Playbook for Marketers, a standalone module
  • A term-long capstone framework where learners built autonomous AI agents and automation pipelines
  • Live instruction for select sessions, plus the capstone briefing and full-course revision materials

The struggle

One class, two worlds. Some students had never used AI. Others were already building. Most were marketers, not coders, and the capstone still asked every one of them to ship an autonomous agent.

How I handled it

I built the course in layers: AI fluency first, then prompting, then no-code tools, and agents last. Every lesson was tied to a real marketing task, so beginners never got lost and advanced students always had something to build.

What the course covers

AI agent designPrompt engineeringZapier, Make and n8nVibe codingAI-driven SEOAI-powered outreachAI Fluency PlaybookCapstone: ship an agent

The proof

15professionals in the cohort
11weeks of curriculum

Each learner designed and shipped their own AI agent using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Students then took their agents back to work and used them on real marketing tasks.

What I learned

People don't fear AI because it's hard. They fear it because nobody showed them the first step. A clear first win changes everything.

What is the difference between a social media manager and a content strategist?

A social media manager runs channels day to day: posting, replying and reporting. A content strategist decides what to say, to whom and why, across every channel. I've done both, so I can build the plan and then make it happen.
